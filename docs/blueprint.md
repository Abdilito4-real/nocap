# **App Name**: NoCap

## Core Features:

- User Authentication: Sign up / log in with email and password, or Google OAuth.
- Campus Video Feed: TikTok-style vertical video feed showing user-generated content from students. Users can like, comment, and share the videos.
- AI Video Summarization Tool: An AI tool analyzes and summarizes video content to provide quick insights. LLM analyzes the video's transcript (generated through speech-to-text) and identifies key points, summarizing them into a concise overview for users. This is a tool.
- Assignment Tracker: Students can add, view, and manage assignments with due dates and course codes. Push notifications for upcoming deadlines.
- Student Job Board: Browse and search part-time jobs, internships, and gigs specifically for students. Job postings include title, company, location, description, and apply link.
- Anonymous Confessions: A separate feed where students can post anonymously. Posts can be text, images, or videos, and can be upvoted/downvoted or commented on anonymously. Moderation tools to prevent abuse.
- User Profile: Profile section includes a profile picture, name, university, course, year, and bio. Tabs for user's videos, saved jobs, and assignments.

## Style Guidelines:

- Dark Mode/Black, vibrant and positivity. Primary color: Vibrant blue (#2563EB) for buttons, active states, and highlights, inspired by a sense of energy and connectivity.
- Background color: Light grey (#F9FAFB) for cards, to ensure a modern and clean look.
- Accent color: Purple (#8B5CF6) for anonymous mode indicators. Chosen as an analogous color that conveys a sense of privacy.
- Font family: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a clean, modern, and readable feel in both headings and body text.
- Feather icons (outline style), consistent 24x24.
- 8-point grid (8, 16, 24, 32px margins/padding).
- Cards: Light grey background, rounded corners (12px), subtle shadow.
